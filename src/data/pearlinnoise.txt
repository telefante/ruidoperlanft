Perlin noise, a gradient noise developed by Ken Perlin in 1983
Used to create natural-looking textures and animations
Generates smooth, continuous pseudo-random patterns
Essential in procedural generation of terrain, clouds, and motion
Combines multiple frequencies to create complex, organic patterns
Winner of Academy Award for Technical Achievement in 1997
Fundamental algorithm in computer graphics and game development
Creates coherent noise by interpolating between grid points
Used in both 2D and 3D applications for realistic effects
Revolutionized the field of procedural texture generation
